Site preparation built around the finished project

Good preparation helps the next stage start on sound ground. Parker Plant & Machinery can assess access, levels, drainage and material requirements before carrying out the practical groundwork needed for a clean, stable working area.

Clearance

Remove vegetation, unsuitable material and obstructions before construction or landscaping begins.

Excavation

Dig out areas for driveways, patios, foundations, bases, trenches and level changes.

Drainage

Prepare suitable falls, drainage trenches and soakaway areas to help manage surface water.

Sub-base

Prepare and compact the ground ready for the specified driveway, patio or structural build-up.

Preparation for driveways, patios and landscaping

We can prepare access routes, remove soft ground, establish levels and form the base before surfacing, paving, decking or landscaping work. The scope is agreed around the intended finish rather than applying the same approach to every site.

Foundations, bases and service trenches

Site preparation can include foundation excavation, concrete base preparation and trenches for drainage or services. Where access is restricted, our mini digger hire Cornwall service can provide controlled excavation with operator hire available.
